# Databricks App and Lakebase Resources

## Knowledge

- [Databricks Free Edition limitations](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/getting-started/free-edition-limitations)
  Authoritative limits for Apps, Lakebase projects, quotas, scale-to-zero, and runtime shutdown. Use before creating resources or preparing for grading.
- [Using Lakebase with Databricks Apps](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/oltp/projects/databricks-apps)
  First-party overview of connecting an App to Lakebase and the lifecycle relationship between app compute and persistent data.
- [Add a Lakebase resource to a Databricks App](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/dev-tools/databricks-apps/lakebase)
  Use when attaching the database, granting the app service principal access, and identifying injected PostgreSQL environment variables.
- [Deploy a Databricks App](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/dev-tools/databricks-apps/deploy)
  First-party Git-source and manual deployment instructions.
- [Lakebase OAuth credential rotation tutorial](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/oltp/projects/tutorial-databricks-apps-autoscaling)
  Use when implementing renewable database credentials instead of storing a password.
- [Databricks App forwarded identity headers](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/dev-tools/databricks-apps/http-headers)
  Reference for attributing tickets and messages to the signed-in user.
